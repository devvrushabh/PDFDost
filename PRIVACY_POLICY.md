## 1. Overview & Core Philosophy

**PDFDost** is an offline-first, client-side document scanner and PDF utility developed independently by **Vrushabh Dhote**.

We respect your personal privacy. **PDFDost does NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII), scanned documents, photos, or usage data.**

All image processing, document conversions (Images to PDF, Word to PDF, PDF to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, etc.), merging, splitting, watermarking, and password locking execute **100% locally on your device**. No documents or images ever leave your device.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

- ❌ **No Account Credentials**: No registration, no username, password, email, or phone number required.
- ❌ **No Document Uploads**: Your files are stored in your device's private sandbox storage only.
- ❌ **No Analytics or Trackers**: Zero third-party telemetry, analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Flurry, no Meta SDK).
- ❌ **No Advertising IDs**: Zero advertisements; we do not read or access your Google Advertising ID.
- ❌ **No Location Data**: No GPS or coarse location permissions requested.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

PDFDost requests minimal runtime permissions strictly required for document operations:

| Permission | Purpose | Data Handling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`CAMERA`** | Required when taking photos to scan documents with the camera. | Captured photos are processed in memory and saved to local storage. Never uploaded. |
| **`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`** / **`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`** | Required to pick photos, Word documents, or PDFs from your gallery or file manager. | Only the specific files chosen by the user are read locally. |
| **`WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`** | Required on Android 9 and below to save exported PDFs to the Downloads folder. | File creation is strictly local. |
| **`INTERNET`** | Optional; used solely when clicking external links (e.g. GitHub repo, email support, or launching UPI payment apps). | No document or user telemetry is transmitted. |

---

## 4. 30-Day Trash & Data Retention

- Files created in the app remain entirely on your device filesystem.
- When an item is deleted, it is moved to a local **Trash** folder and retained for **30 days** before permanent removal.
- You can restore files at any time during this 30-day window or permanently purge them immediately using **"Empty Trash"**.
- Uninstalling the application deletes all internal sandbox data immediately.

## 5. Voluntary UPI Donations

PDFDost is completely free. In Settings, users may optionally support development via UPI (`8830638458-2@ybl`). This opens standard Android `upi://pay` intents to delegate to your installed payment app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm). PDFDost does not process, handle, or store any banking, card, or UPI details.

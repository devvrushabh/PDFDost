Privacy Policy for PDFDost

PDFDost is engineered from the ground up with a zero-cloud, offline-first architecture. We believe your private documents, scanned papers, and personal records should remain exclusively on your device.

Application PDFDost (com.devvrushabh.pdfdost)
Developer Vrushabh Dhote
Effective Date October 15, 2026
Store Compliance Indus Appstore & Google Play

🔒 1. Core Privacy Commitment

PDFDost ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed independently by Vrushabh Dhote, operates strictly as an offline client-side utility application.

Summary: PDFDost does NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII), documents, photos, or device data with any remote servers or third-party entities.

All image-to-PDF conversions, document scanning, merging, splitting, watermarking, password encryption, and file manipulations are executed 100% locally on your smartphone processor using on-device Flutter and native Android runtime environments.

🚫 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure transparency, PDFDost explicitly clarifies that it does NOT collect:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email address, physical address, or account credentials.
  • No Document Uploads: Any PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or photo imported into the app remains strictly stored in your local device sandbox.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: The app contains no third-party tracking SDKs (no Google Firebase Analytics, no Meta/Facebook SDKs, no Mixpanel, and no Flurry).
  • No Advertising IDs: The app is 100% free and ad-free. We do not read Google Advertising ID (AAID) or track user behaviors.
  • No Location Data: The app does not access your GPS coordinates, coarse location, or Wi-Fi network identifiers.

📱 3. Android Device Permissions & Usage

PDFDost requests minimal Android runtime permissions strictly required to perform document utility tasks. Every permission is requested dynamically only when you invoke the corresponding feature:

Permission Feature & Purpose Data Handling
CAMERA Used when you tap "Scan with Camera" or capture pages to create a new document set. Frames and photos are processed in memory and saved to your device's local private storage. Never uploaded.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Used when you tap "Import Images" or select documents from your gallery to create PDFs. Only files explicitly selected by you through Android's system file picker are read locally.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
(Android < 10)		 Used on legacy devices to export and save generated PDF files to your device's Downloads or Documents directory. File creation is strictly local.
INTERNET Used solely when the user clicks an external link (such as viewing source code on GitHub, opening email to contact support, or triggering a UPI payment app). No app document data or personal telemetry is transmitted over the network.

🗑️ 4. Data Storage, Retention & 30-Day Trash Policy

All documents, sets, folders, and history records created within PDFDost are saved locally in the app's private sandbox storage (via SharedPreferences and device local storage).

  • 30-Day Trash Auto-Retention: When you delete a document or conversion history item, it is moved to an on-device Trash folder with a retention timestamp. Files in the Trash are kept for 30 days to protect you from accidental deletion.
  • User Restoration: You can restore any item from the Trash back to its active folder with 1 tap at any time within the 30-day window.
  • Permanent Deletion: Files older than 30 days are automatically deleted permanently from your device storage. You can also tap "Empty Trash" or "Delete Permanently" at any time to immediately erase them.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the app completely purges all app-private storage, trash, and temporary caches from your Android device.

💳 5. Voluntary UPI Donations

PDFDost is 100% free with no in-app purchases or paywalls. In the Settings tab, users may optionally support open-source development through voluntary UPI donations:

  • Tapping "Donate and Pay" or scanning the QR code invokes Android's standard upi://pay system intent to hand over the transaction directly to your installed UPI banking application (e.g., PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM).
  • PDFDost does NOT collect, process, or store bank account details, UPI PINs, credit card numbers, or transaction histories. All financial transactions are handled solely by your bank and chosen UPI payment provider.

👶 6. Children's Privacy

PDFDost is a general utility app suitable for all age groups. Because the app does not collect any personal data, register accounts, or transmit information over the internet, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 in compliance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and applicable global regulations.

🛡️ 7. Data Security

We rely on the robust security mechanisms provided by the Android Operating System, including Linux application sandboxing and scoped storage. Other applications installed on your device cannot access PDFDost's internal storage without explicit user interaction.

📝 8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or platform requirements. Any updates will be published on this live URL with an updated "Effective Date". Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of any revisions.
